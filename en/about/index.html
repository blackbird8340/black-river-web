About us

The value of
choosing well

Black River Properties was founded on a simple conviction: buying or selling a property is one of the most important decisions in a person’s life, and it deserves to be guided with professionalism, honesty and trust.

We are an independent real estate advisory firm specialising in the coast of Maldonado and Rocha, from Solís to Punta del Diablo.

We personally guide both those searching for the right property and those looking to sell, invest or develop new opportunities in Uruguay.

Our approach combines market knowledge, personal attention and a solid network of professionals — licensed brokers, developers and owners — that allows us to support every transaction from end to end.

At Black River Properties we believe our work goes far beyond connecting people with properties. It is about understanding what each client is looking for, advising them with clear information, and making the whole process simple, secure and transparent from beginning to end.

Commitment

Our starting point is always the client. We listen, we understand what you are looking for, and we analyse the market to bring you the opportunities that best fit your goals.

Discretion

We work with the discretion that significant transactions demand, protecting the privacy of buyers and owners alike.

Closeness

We personally accompany every stage: from the first enquiry to the closing, and beyond.
